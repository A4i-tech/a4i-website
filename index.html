ABOUT A4I

Making AI Accessible for All 

The AI Innovation & Inclusion Initiative (A4I) is a collaboration between Microsoft India and the International Institute of Information Technology Bangalore. 

A4I is committed to ensuring that the benefits of artificial intelligence reach the furthest corners of society, especially the underserved and marginalized communities. Our work focuses on researching, developing, deploying, and scaling Digital Public Goods (DPGs) : free, open, and accessible AI solutions designed to address real-world challenges faced by underprivileged groups. 

From AI-powered educational tools that support visually impaired learners to intelligent chatbots that provide reliable, timely medical information to ASHA workers, A4I builds technology with purpose. These solutions strengthen communities from the ground up by enhancing access to knowledge, healthcare, education, and more.  

We believe technology is most powerful when it is inclusive. Our mission is to leverage cutting-edge artificial intelligence to drive social inclusion and sustainable impact.

Partners & PROJECTS

Khushi Baby Association

Khushi Baby is a nonprofit health technology organization working to strengthen rural healthcare delivery across India. It has supported over 11 million beneficiaries and empowered 70,000+ frontline health workers across multiple states.

In partnership with Khushi Baby, A4I is scaling AshaBot—an AI-powered WhatsApp chatbot that provides ASHA workers with instant, reliable answers to their health-related queries, improving last-mile healthcare support.

Partners & PROJECTS

Vision Empower Trust

Vision Empower Trust is a Karnataka-based nonprofit incubated at IIIT Bangalore, dedicated to empowering children with visual impairments through inclusive STEM education.

Working across 15 Indian states, the organization supports over 3,000 visually impaired students and 1,555 teachers across 134 schools.

In partnership with Vision Empower Trust, A4I has introduced SEEDS – a voice-based educational platform designed specifically for visually impaired students and teachers, delivering accessible and engaging learning experiences at scale.

Partners & PROJECTS

Sikshana Foundation

Established in 2002 and based in Bangalore, Sikshana Foundation works to improve learning outcomes in government primary schools across Karnataka. The organization partners with state education departments, communities, and volunteers to strengthen foundational literacy and numeracy, teacher training, and ICT integration.

In partnership with Sikshana Foundation, A4I is scaling Siksha Copilot- an AI-powered teaching assistant that supports educators with real-time lesson planning, automated remedial content, and personalized feedback to enhance classroom effectiveness.

Innovation Flow

Intelligence
Scaled for Good

A4I partners with leading organizations in education and healthcare to co-design and scale high-impact AI solutions for underserved communities. As part of the Microsoft Elevate Program, we build on Microsoft ‘solutions, intentionally scaling them to reach the people who need them most. 

This impact is made possible through close collaboration with our domain partners, who implement the solutions on the ground, train users, and help identify the right communities. Together, we create strong feedback loops that enable continuous improvement and ensure our Digital Public Goods evolve in response to real-world needs.
